Sadhana · daily practice

Teaching and Researchಸಾಧನೆ

Practice is never finished. He teaches, studies, recites and researches, all as one sadhana.

Teaching

Faculty, SaPa Internationalಸಪ ಇಂಟರ್‌ನ್ಯಾಷನಲ್

Kousthubh is a prominent faculty member at SaPa International, where he teaches Carnatic music to many aspiring and gifted students.

He teaches the way he was taught: patiently, with attention to sahitya and pronunciation, to shruthi and to the shape of each phrase. For students who want to begin or deepen their study, he is glad to talk.

Research

Raga Iconificationರಾಗ ಪ್ರತಿಮೀಕರಣ

He is currently working on projects in Raga Iconification and on the special musical compositions of Karnataka composers. The sketch below is only a playful illustration, not the research itself: each raga drawn as a shape on the wheel of twelve swaras.

Mayamalavagowla

Tap a raga to hear its arohana, plucked like a tambura string.

Gamaka

The art of the sung epicಗಮಕ ಕಲೆ

Gamaka is Karnataka's own art of singing and explaining epic poetry. Kousthubh has passed all four examinations in gamaka with first class.

PrathamaPraveshaProudhaKaajaana

Veda, shloka and stotra

The Bhagavadgita, Samskrutha shlokas and the Soundaryalahari learned from his grandmother; the Vedas learned under Veda Vidwan H.S. Balakrishna; and today, the Adhyatma Geethavali of the Adhyatma Prakashana Karyalaya. Each one feeds his singing.
