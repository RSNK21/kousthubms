01

Roots in Mysuruಮೈಸೂರಿನ ಬೇರು

Kousthubh was born on 28 August 1996 in Mysuru, the cultural capital of Karnataka. His family traces its ancestry to the historic Halli Mysore. From his childhood, his parents, Sri H.N. Subbanna and Smt. H.N. Nagalakshmi, made sure he received an education that would grow his love for music, art and Indian values.

That gentle, rooted upbringing is still the first thing people notice about him: a calm and serene presence, on stage and off it.

02

A grandmother's voiceಅಜ್ಜಿಯ ಧ್ವನಿ

His first teacher was his grandmother, Smt. Kaveramma. From the age of five, he has recited what she taught him: the Srimad Bhagavadgita, Samskrutha shlokas and the Soundaryalahari. As a child he won more than twenty prizes at Bhagavadgita recital competitions across Mysuru.

He also studied the Vedas under Veda Vidwan H.S. Balakrishna. The clarity of text and pronunciation he learned here lives in every kriti he sings today.

03

Two disciplines, one mindಎರಡು ಶಿಸ್ತು, ಒಂದೇ ಮನಸ್ಸು

He completed his foundational music training under Smt. Jyothi Shankar, and in 2019 secured a distinction and the first rank in the Board's Senior grade examination in Karnataka Music (Vocal), KSEEB, Government of Karnataka.

In the same year he earned his M.C.A. with distinction from The National Institute of Engineering, Mysuru (VTU, Belagavi). Logic and laya, code and kriti: he has never seen them as opposites.

04

In the gurukulaಗುರುಕುಲದಲ್ಲಿ

In 2024 he completed his Master of Music (M.A. in Music) with an A+ grade at the Rasarishi Institution, affiliated to Hampi Kannada University, under the guidance of the eminent musician and musicologist, Sangeetha Vidwan Dr. R.S. Nandakumar.

He accompanies his guru in all his concerts, providing shruthi and vocal support, and continues to learn in the old and disciplined way of the Gurukula.

05

A voice on the airಆಕಾಶವಾಣಿಯ ಕಂಠ

Akashavani, All India Radio, first recognised him in 2015 with a first prize in folk music. In 2025, All India Radio Mysuru graded him a B High Grade Artist (Prasar Bharati).

His songs have been featured in the award winning All India Radio programme Vachana Pada Vaidya, and on SVBC television in Nada Neerajanam, Tirupati.

06

Teacher and seekerಗುರು ಮತ್ತು ಸಾಧಕ

He is a prominent faculty member at SaPa International, where he teaches Carnatic music to many gifted students. He is now learning bhajans and philosophical compositions such as the Adhyatma Geethavali of the Adhyatma Prakashana Karyalaya of His Holiness Sri Sacchidanandendra Saraswathi Swami.

His current research looks at Raga Iconification and at the special compositions of Karnataka composers.

His aspiration

Music that helps a whole culture grow

Kousthubh is building his career with one wish: that his music should foster all round cultural and holistic development. People who hear him speak of the richness of his voice, his innate sense of proportion and the feeling he brings to every phrase. He hopes to be a vocalist admired as much for his adherence to Indian traditions and values as for his singing.
